## Mechanism review

Lifecycle ordering and pending leases own already selected native instances. The independent lifecycle consumer needs its required provider to start first and to remain acquired until consumer cleanup finishes. A plugin cannot own other loader images or safely roll back the kernel's acquisitions. The resolver's existing required graph supplies the order; annotations and injected behavior remain in SDK plugins. There is no new dispatcher, provider resolution path, scheduler or authority owner.

Next tasks are dependency ordered: accept all eleven M0.2 gates and record exact-head CI; implement the independently regression-tested M0.3 generation contract; complete M0.4 conformance.
